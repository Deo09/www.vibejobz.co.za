About VibeJobz

VibeJobz is a free portal that helps South African youth find jobs, learnerships, bursaries, free courses and matric resources, all in one place.

Why we built it

Finding real opportunities in South Africa is hard. Listings are scattered across many sites, deadlines are easy to miss, and scams target people who are simply looking for a start. VibeJobz brings opportunities together so you can search, compare and apply without paying anything.

What you will find here

  • Jobs: vacancies across all nine provinces, filterable by province, type and category.
  • Courses: free certificates and training in IT, finance, coding and digital skills.
  • After Matric Hub: an APS Calculator, university portals, study abroad scholarships and local bursaries.
  • Closing Soon: learnerships, bursaries and internships with deadlines in the next 48 hours.
  • Matric study materials: past papers and study guides.

Our promise

  • Using VibeJobz is free. We will never ask you to pay to see or apply for an opportunity.
  • We link to official application pages wherever possible.
  • We remove listings that turn out to be fake, expired or misleading.
