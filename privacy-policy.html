Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

VibeJobz ("we", "us") runs vibejobz.co.za, a free portal for jobs, courses, bursaries and matric resources in South Africa. This policy explains what information we handle and your choices. We aim to follow South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Information we collect

Information you give us: if you email or phone us, we receive your contact details and message. We use them only to reply.

Information collected automatically: like most websites, we and our service providers may collect your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and time spent. We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used.

Information stored on your device: our APS Calculator saves your marks in your browser's local storage on your own phone or computer. We do not receive or store these marks on our servers.

How we use information

To run and improve the site, understand which content is useful, answer your messages, keep the site secure, and show advertising that helps keep VibeJobz free.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We and our partners use them to remember settings, measure traffic and show ads. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features may stop working.

Google advertising and AdSense

We may use Google AdSense to display ads. Google and other third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings. You can also learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run the site, including hosting and database services (such as Supabase), Google Analytics and Google AdSense. They process data only as needed to provide their services and under their own privacy policies.

Third-party links

Our listings link to employers, universities, bursary providers and other external sites, and to our WhatsApp Channel. We do not control these sites and are not responsible for their privacy practices. Read their policies before sharing personal information.

Children

Many of our visitors are matric learners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18 without a parent or guardian's consent, and we do not ask you to create an account to use the site.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask to see, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or object to its use. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no online system is completely secure. We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
